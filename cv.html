Profile

Consultant in AI safety and responsible AI, focused on human oversight, transparency and trust in AI systems. Twenty-eight years at IBM Research in human-computer interaction, software engineering and AI; in the later years, led the human-centered design of the Trusted AI toolkits (AI Fairness 360, AI Explainability 360 and AI FactSheets) and chaired the Exploratory Computer Science Council. The fairness and explainability toolkits became the leading open-source tools in their area and were transferred to the Linux Foundation in 2020; FactSheets became the core of IBM's AI governance product and was rated the most complete of six documentation approaches against the EU AI Act in a study by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (Hupont et al., Documenting High-Risk AI: A European Regulatory Perspective, IEEE Computer, 2023). Recent work focuses on the design of agentic and generative AI assistants that support, rather than replace, human experts, notably chemists searching for alternatives to PFAS "forever chemicals". Contributor to AI governance work at the Partnership on AI, IEEE and the Computing Research Association.
